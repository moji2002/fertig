Why another classless sheet

Most classless stylesheets are either too plain to ship or too heavy to justify. fertig aims at the gap between them: opinionated enough that the page looks finished on the first load, small enough that you never think about it again.

It carries nothing for browsers that are already gone. No vendor prefixes, no polyfills, no fallback stack for features that shipped years ago — every rule in it is written the way the platform works now.

  • 01

    Bidirectional. Logical properties throughout, so dir="rtl" mirrors the entire sheet rather than patching it.

  • 02

    Classless. Semantic tags are styled directly. Seven optional classes exist; you can ignore every one.

  • 03

    Modern only. OKLCH, light-dark(), cascade layers, anchor positioning, container queries.

  • 04

    Accessible. Every colour pair clears WCAG AA in both themes — measured, not assumed.
