Why another classless sheet

Most are either too plain to ship or too heavy to justify. fertig aims at the gap: opinionated enough to look finished, small enough that you never think about it again.

Bidirectional

Logical properties throughout, so dir="rtl" mirrors the entire sheet rather than patching it.

Classless

Semantic tags are styled directly. Seven optional classes exist; you can ignore every one.

Modern only

OKLCH, light-dark(), cascade layers, anchor positioning, container queries.

Accessible

Every colour pair clears WCAG AA in both themes, measured rather than assumed.
